Four Pillars of Destiny

Traditional Saju Analysis

In Eastern philosophy, Saju (Four Pillars of Destiny) is a traditional way to understand a person's natural balance by analyzing the five elements—wood, fire, earth, metal, and water—present at their birth. Each element represents different energies of life, such as growth, passion, stability, strength, and flow. When these elements are in harmony, it suggests physical, emotional, and spiritual wellness. Rather than predicting fate, Saju offers insight into how one can restore balance and live in alignment with their natural energy.
